How the community record works

Competitions share one calendar. Recorded results distinguish live standings from finalized winners.

Shared wins count for every tied winner. Trophy totals cover the available retained history. Reconstructed and partial periods are labeled, and days without studying have no winner. Study-day awards count studying, not streak freezes.
